About NIST University

Odisha's first NRI higher-education venture — a 65-acre research campus in the Pallur hills since 1996.

30+ Years of
Excellence

National Institute of Science & Technology

NIST University, located at Institute Park, Berhampur, Odisha, was established in 1996 as the first NRI higher-education venture in Odisha, promoted by the SM Charitable Educational Trust. Situated in the lush green hills of Pallur, the campus spreads across a 65-acre expanse.

NIST provides world-class education and research opportunities across Engineering, Science, Management and Health Sciences. The university has developed Centers of Research Excellence and Global Innovation Centers to support multidisciplinary research, entrepreneurship and collaboration with industries and universities worldwide.

🔭 Vision

“Focused on High Quality Learning, Creative Innovation, Entrepreneurship, and Universal Partnership.”

🎯 Mission

World-Class Facilities

A 65-acre green campus with state-of-the-art academic infrastructure, research centers, residential halls and sports amenities.

Computer Lab

State-of-the-art computing laboratories supporting practical learning, experimentation and cutting-edge research with high-performance systems and licensed software.

Central Library

The Central Library provides students access to books, journals and electronic resources to support academic learning and research across all disciplines.

Smart Classrooms

Modern classrooms with interactive displays and technology-enabled infrastructure support dynamic, hybrid and collaborative learning across the campus.

Hostel

Separate residential facilities for boys and girls including Albert Einstein Hall, Rabindranath Tagore Hall and Marie Curie Hall with Wi-Fi, dining, study areas and security.

Sports Complex

Cricket/football grounds, basketball courts, squash, tennis, swimming pool, indoor sports pavilion, badminton courts and a gymnasium for holistic student development.
